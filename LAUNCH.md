# Launch playbook: your first 100 paying customers

The product is finished. Revenue now depends on putting it in front of sellers who are tired of writing listings. This is a practical plan, in order.

## Who you're selling to

**Start with Etsy sellers.** They write many listings, obsess over titles and tags, gather in public communities, and usually have no copywriter. Amazon, Shopify and eBay sellers are the next wave, and the product already supports them.

**Their pain, in their words:** "I have 40 new products and hate writing descriptions." "My listings get no views." "I don't know what tags to use." Use these phrases in posts and ads.

**Your pitch in one line:** *Paste your product notes and get an Etsy-ready title, description and all 13 tags in 20 seconds. First 5 free.*

## Week 1: get 10 real users

1. **Deploy and buy your own Starter pack** with a live card to prove the money path works. Then refund it.
2. **Rewrite 10 real listings for free.** Find Etsy shops with weak titles, run their product through the app, and send the owner the result: "I rewrote your [product] listing with a tool I built. Want to try it on the rest of your shop? First 5 are free." Personal, specific and useful beats any ad.
3. **Collect before/after examples.** A screenshot of an old listing next to the new one is your best marketing asset. Ask the 10 users for a one-line quote.

## Weeks 2–4: communities (free, highest intent)

Be a helpful seller first and a founder second. Most communities ban drive-by self-promotion.

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| r/EtsySellers, r/Etsy | Answer "why no views?" threads with real SEO advice, and mention the tool only where it fits. Post a before/after case study. |
| r/AmazonSeller, r/FulfillmentByAmazon | Share the Amazon title and backend-search-term rules as a guide, and link the tool at the end. |
| r/shopify, r/ecommerce | "Built a product description generator, here's what I learned about product page copy." |
| r/Flipping, r/eBay | Resellers list hundreds of items. Pitch speed: "80-character eBay titles in 10 seconds." |
| Facebook groups for Etsy and handmade sellers | Offer free listing rewrites in the comments, and ask admins for permission to post a launch offer. |
| Indie Hackers, Product Hunt | Post a launch story with real numbers. This brings early adopters and backlinks. |

## Month 2+: channels that compound

1. **Short video (TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts).** Film a screen recording: messy notes go in, a polished listing comes out, a countdown timer, then "link in bio." Seller-side TikTok is large and cheap to reach. Post 3–5 a week.
2. **Free SEO tools** (see the README roadmap). Pages like "Etsy tag length checker" or "Amazon title character counter" rank for searches sellers already make, and each one has a "write the whole listing with AI" button.
3. **Blog posts that answer seller questions,** such as "How to write Etsy titles in 2026", "13 Etsy tag ideas for [category]" or "Amazon backend search terms explained". Every post ends with the free-trial call to action.
4. **Affiliates.** Etsy coaches and seller YouTubers have audiences that trust them. Give each one a Stripe promotion code (for example 20% off for their audience), and pay them 30% of referred revenue.
5. **Paid ads, only once conversion is proven.** Test Pinterest and Meta ads aimed at "Etsy seller" interests. Keep ads running only while revenue per signup exceeds cost per signup.

## Numbers to watch (all visible in `/admin`)

| Metric | Healthy target | If it's low |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor → signup | 5–15% | Rewrite the hero headline, add real before/after examples |
| Signup → first listing | 60%+ | Pre-fill an example product, simplify the form |
| Free → paid | 3–8% | Try 3 free credits instead of 5, add a small $5 pack, email users on their last free credit |
| Repeat purchase | 20%+ | Send a "your shop has new products?" email after 30 days |
| AI cost per listing | < $0.06 | Lower `CLAUDE_EFFORT` or trim the prompt |

## Pricing experiments worth running

- **Anchor higher.** A $249 "Agency" pack with 2,000 credits makes the $99 pack look reasonable.
- **Launch discount.** Create a Stripe promotion code like `LAUNCH30` for early communities.
- **Subscription test.** Some sellers prefer $19/month for 150 listings. Test it once you have 50 paying customers.

## Rules that keep you out of trouble

- Don't spam communities or DMs. A ban costs more than the sale.
- Don't claim guaranteed rankings or sales. Say "optimized for search", never "rank #1".
- Keep the Terms and Privacy pages accurate, and honor refund requests quickly. Chargebacks hurt your Stripe account.
- Answer support email within a day. At this stage, every happy customer is a testimonial.
