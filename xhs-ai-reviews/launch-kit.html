Months 1–6

The road to paid reviews

Brands pay for reviews through 蒲公英, 小红书's official deal platform. It opens at 1,000 followers. Everything before that builds the two things brands pay for: an audience in 科技数码, and readers who trust your verdicts.

Stage 1 · now to 1,000 followers

Build the AI series

Something launches almost every week, so you're never short of a timely post. The tools are free, so readers can try along. AI apps are also among 小红书's biggest advertisers. Use the same method every time (same prompts, trap checks, screenshots) so your results become known as the ones to trust.

Stage 2 · 1,000 followers

Open 蒲公英 and take the first deals

Set your price. The platform minimum is ¥100, image posts from small accounts usually go for a few hundred yuan, and video pays about twice as much. Apply for 新品试用 (brands send you products to test). It's the easiest way into app and gadget reviews. Only accept products you'd test with your normal method.

Stage 3 · beyond

Widen to apps and gadgets

Take on other tech categories as brands ask, using the same scorecard style. From around week 3, try short screen-recorded videos, since video deals pay more. Keep sponsored posts a minority, and run every one through 蒲公英 so it's labelled. 小红书 penalises paid posts arranged privately (私单).

Every post

Rules that protect a new account

小红书 tightened its AI rules in 2026: accounts that post fully AI-written notes get banned, and it wants AI-assisted posts labelled. Labelled posts that follow the rules aren't penalised in reach.

Declare AI assistance when publishing.In the publish screen's content declaration (内容类型声明), pick the AI option whenever I helped write the text.
Keep it first-hand.Your own screenshots and recordings, your verdicts, and at least one line in your own words. That's the value, and it's what keeps the account safe.
Reply to every comment in the first hour.Early comments and replies tell the feed the post is worth showing to more people.
No WeChat IDs, QR codes, links or other platforms' names.Sending people off 小红书 (导流) gets posts limited, even in comments and replies.
Don't explain how to reach blocked foreign AI, and don't sell access.Mentioning foreign models as news or in comparisons is fine. Keep the tests on apps people in China can use.
Never claim a result you didn't test.If something wasn't tried, the post says so or leaves it out.
Don't use other people's images, and don't buy followers or likes.Both get new accounts limited quickly.
